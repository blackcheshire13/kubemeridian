I'm a **DevOps and platform engineer who also writes the tools**. I have spent 13+ years
in IT infrastructure, eight of them in DevOps: Kubernetes on AWS and bare metal, GitOps with
Argo CD, Terraform, Istio, Vault, and the Prometheus, Grafana, Loki and Tempo stack. One
habit came out of it: when something in production annoys me twice, I build the fix.

## Track record

- Consolidated 10+ Kubernetes clusters across four clouds into four, under one
  hub-and-spoke Argo CD control plane — [the write-up](/blog/consolidating-kubernetes-clusters).
- Scaled a crypto exchange from 2–3k to 15–20k requests per second on EKS and bare-metal
  Kubernetes, with zero-downtime releases.
- Ran Kubernetes in air-gapped enterprise environments under strict infosec and compliance
  rules.
- Since July 2026, building ICE and deusex.

## What I'm building

- **[ICE](/ice/)** — active defence for Kubernetes. It turns findings and drift into GitOps
  pull requests a person approves, and keeps a case open until a fresh measurement proves
  the fix. In development; looking for [design partners](/ice/design-partners).
- **[deusex](/deusex/)** — an autonomous development loop that plans, builds, reviews and
  merges code on a schedule and stops only at the decisions people have not handed over.
  It develops ICE, and itself.
- **[KubeMeridian](/kubemeridian)** — Kubernetes observability for Grafana. Archived in
  October 2026.

## Work with me

Design-partner pilots for ICE, conversations about autonomous development with deusex,
contract work on Kubernetes, observability, cost and SLOs — or just comparing notes on
platform engineering.
